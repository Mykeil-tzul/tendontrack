What it measures

Google MediaPipe estimates the position of your ankle, heel and toe. The app measures how the line from heel to toe tilts compared with the first moment of the clip. That camera-based estimate is not clinical range of motion.

What stays on this device

Video and frame data stay in memory. If you save a session, only its summary metrics go into this browser’s local storage. Anyone who shares this browser profile may be able to see those summaries.

Reading the numbers

Peak lift is the median of detected rep peaks. Rep spread shows how far apart the middle half of peaks are. Changes can come from camera placement or model error as well as movement.

Why I built TendonTrack

I ruptured my Achilles in June 2026, and it changed my life. I love basketball and played professionally. Recovery made me realize how many people, from athletes across different sports to people who have never competed, go through this injury. I built TendonTrack to help someone else see their progress from one rehab session to the next.
